## Shared border on six top boards

All five designs and both Kumiko variants have one outer frame, four stack-hole rims, and four rail-slot rims. These nine closed strokes are flat top-side copper and mask artwork. They are not applied to lower boards.

![Gold frames on all top boards](/assets/pcb-art/images/pcb-art-top-gold-borders.png)

| Feature | Shape and dimensions |
| --- | --- |
| Outer frame | Centerline 0.55 mm inside the board edge; 0.32 mm line width |
| Stack-hole rim | 2.70 mm centerline radius; 0.40 mm line width |
| Rail-slot rim | 2.10 mm offset from slot axis; 0.40 mm line width |
| Spider and Kumiko | Octagonal stack rims and rectangular slot rims |
| Other designs | Circular stack rims and capsule-shaped slot rims |

The slot rims join the outer frame where they meet. These graphics do not specify plated holes or plated board edges.

## Viewer art and export candidate

The dimensions describe the Revision 5 viewer art. The native-board export applies a 0.35 mm mask-opening clearance from cut edges and drills, so artwork near those boundaries is clipped. Review the result in the [gold-area comparison](../manufacturing/copper-review.mdx).

Fastener heads and washers may cover part of a rim. Confirm that against actual hardware. The source confirms the border geometry is present in all six top variants; it does not decide hardware or supplier rules.
