White and black layers overlap to reveal seven different maze paths below the top. Keep their routes distinct instead of reusing one shifted pattern. Revision 4 spread the routes into a larger region and increased top opening area by about 47.2%. Revision 5 adds a gold frame, hole rims, and top maze artwork. No gold pattern is added to the lower mask-only boards.

![Top eight Woven Maze layers](/assets/pcb-art/images/woven-maze-layers-01-08.png)

## Review each layer

Use the isolated-layer preview, then return to the assembled view. Check where broad white bands hide black ones and how the windows reveal deeper routes. Every board still needs a connected perimeter and mounting supports.
