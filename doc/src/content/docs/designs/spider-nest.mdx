The irregular web extends close to the perimeter. Twelve main radial strands reach outward, four of them connected to mounting supports. Cross strands shift between layers, leaving a deep view toward the red bottom. The top copper follows the web outward rather than adding a uniform tiled pattern. Revision 5 adds the shared top frame and hole rims.

The top opening grew about 29.5% from Revision 3 to Revision 4; Revision 5 preserves that physical opening. White layers and the red bottom are mask-only. Layers 3, 5, and 7 show gold using black mask and exposed ENIG.

## Review points

Check thin strand connections, sharp interior corners, support necks, and visibility of the red bottom. A process adaptation should not sever strands or flatten the web by filling openings.
