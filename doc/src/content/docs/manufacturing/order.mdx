The current quote candidates select **Coral Vault, Fault Line, Kumiko Void wide and Spider Nest**: exactly 34 source boards, with four individually routed cosmetic tops and 30 lowers. Strip Mine has already been ordered and is outside this selection. Woven Maze and standard Kumiko remain historical/reference sources.

The [four-series profile](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/blob/main/artwork-resources/pcb-art/manufacturing/order-profiles/four-art-series.json) and [build/download guide](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/blob/main/scripts/art-order/README.md) define three alternatives. Choose **one** alternative's order list; never purchase every ZIP in the bundle.

| Alternative, 25 stacks per series | Order lines | Sheets / standalone units | Useful pieces | Blank cells |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Individual boards | 34 | 850 | 850 | 0 |
| Grouped, one ten-member red sheet | 12 | 300 | 850 | 50 |

All alternatives supply 100 complete stacks, comprising 100 top and 750 lower pieces. Requested hardware totals are 400 screws, 3,000 spacers and 400 nuts; dimensions and fit assumptions remain unchanged. Profile quantities can be adjusted per series. Supply rounds up to configured supplier sheet quantities and explicitly reports surplus per board ID and complete-stack yield. Confirm offered quantities with the quote.

Purple, yellow and blue remain individual lower boards. Color **and** finish determine compatibility: black ENIG is separate from black HASL. The red six-member sheet holds Spider L08, Coral L05/L07 and Kumiko wide L02/L05/L08. The four-member red sheet holds Fault L02/L04/L06/L08. The split has no blank cells and is the preferred compact candidate for quotation, not a proven cheaper option.

Native source artwork, apertures, mounting holes and stack geometry remain unchanged. Lower sheets use translation-only placement and 5 mm rails. Scores use a separate fabrication drawing; decorative apertures remain routed. Tops retain their finished routed edges and rail slots. The workflow checks actual native/CAM outputs and includes individual/placement previews, nominal stack-side views, DRC logs, package hashes and source/tool/run provenance.

The read-only Actions workflow runs the same checked-in entry point used locally. The [guide](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/blob/main/scripts/art-order/README.md) explains exact-run artifact downloads and checksum/inventory verification. Each variant includes its own purchasing table and machine-readable manifest. The artifact records the PR merge commit as the tested source, with head/base SHAs separately. Later relevant edits need fresh evidence.

Prices and engineering/design, routing/scoring, ENIG, area, shipping and tax charges remain **unknown** until an actual dated quote. Blank cells exclude rails and cutouts. Fewer sheets or order lines do not establish savings. Factory CAM/scoring approval, residual score-web depth, perforated-sheet support, side-edge appearance, finish availability and physical stack/fastener fit remain external checks. Native DRC is not factory approval; these candidates are not a purchase commitment.
