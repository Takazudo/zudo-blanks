`panels/art-grouped-lowers/` preserves the historical **five-series** sheet sources. These five sheets are not the completed four-series cart. The completed archive uses six adopted grouped sheets, including the red6+4 split, together with individual colored lowers and routed tops.

Use the [completed order](/docs/manufacturing/order/) for the exact 13 adopted ZIPs and 34 source-board scope. Do not rebuild or substitute these historical sheets for the frozen archive. Its original fabrication bytes and provenance remain unchanged in ordinary Git.
