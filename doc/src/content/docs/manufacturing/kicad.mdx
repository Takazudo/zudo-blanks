| KiCad feature | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Edge.Cuts` | Routed outer shape and decorative apertures on individual boards; grouped sheets route only their outer profile and decorative apertures |

| NPTH pads | Mechanical stack holes and the top rail slots; exported separately from PTH |

Factory CAM approval, finished board review and physical fit remain external steps.

## Vector interchange and review

Historical Revision 5 SVG uses the common 101.3 × 128.5 mm canvas with lower layers at Y=17.1 mm. DXF uses CAD Y-up (the negated preview Y coordinate). Import at 1:1 millimetres and confirm the origin. `Edge_Cuts` describes routes; Drill vectors are references because native pads define functional holes. F.Cu/F.Mask DXFs are outlines, not filled-area semantics. Never use a rendered `front.svg` as fabrication cut data.

F.Mask records openings, not painted mask; decorative copper has no electrical nets. Check Kumiko's covered black shadow facets separately from exposed gold. Open representative tops and mask-only lowers, save the full DRC result, and compare Gerbers and drills in an independent CAM viewer. Record tool versions and input/output hashes separately from supplier or physical results.
