The selected five art stacks have **43 individual native boards**: Spider Nest 8, Coral Vault 8, Fault Line 9, Kumiko Void **wide** 9, and Woven Maze 9. The nine standard Kumiko boards are reference-only. Local KiCad 10 DRC and CAM checks passed for the selected native boards and five grouped lower sheets. These checks do not constitute factory acceptance.

The [order manifest](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/blob/main/artwork-resources/pcb-art/manufacturing/lower-panels-order-manifest.json) describes 13 packages for 25 complete stacks per design: 325 sheet or standalone order units, yielding 1,075 useful boards and 75 intentionally blank grid cells. It is a planning record. No boards have been purchased or accepted by a manufacturer.

The chosen policy uses two-layer 1.6 mm FR-4 and a 1.0 mm decorative router. The source Revision 5 design needed indexed local adjustments before the native checks. [Read the policy](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/blob/main/artwork-resources/pcb-art/manufacturing/policy.json), [candidate comparison](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/blob/main/artwork-resources/pcb-art/manufacturing/CANDIDATE.md), [individual CAM manifest](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/blob/main/artwork-resources/pcb-art/manufacturing/native-kicad-cam-manifest.json), and [grouped sheet record](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/blob/main/artwork-resources/pcb-art/manufacturing/lower-panels-order-manifest.json) for exact scope.

**Pending external checks:** factory CAM and scoring strategy, actual mask color and finish availability, quote, handling strength, hardware tolerance and physical fit. The exhaustive width certificate is opt-in and has not passed; do not describe it as complete evidence.
