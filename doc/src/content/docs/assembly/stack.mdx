## Assembled dimensions

| Design | Boards | Gaps | 3 mm spacers | Boards plus gaps |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Spider Nest | 8 | 7 | 28 | 33.8 mm |
| Coral Vault | 8 | 7 | 28 | 33.8 mm |
| Fault Line | 9 | 8 | 32 | 38.4 mm |
| Kumiko Void | 9 | 8 | 32 | 38.4 mm |
| Woven Maze | 9 | 8 | 32 | 38.4 mm |

One spacer at each corner of every gap gives 152 spacers and 20 stack screw axes across all five designs. Recalculate if using threaded standoffs or a different support arrangement.

The preview assumes 6 mm outer-diameter spacers. Board tolerances, screw heads, washers, nuts, and thread allowance are not included. Choose final screw lengths against actual hardware and case dimensions.

## Assembly sequence

1. Sort PCBs by design, original board ID, layer number, and side.
2. Stack from the bottom up, adding one 3 mm gap and aligning all four holes.
3. Check the color sequence and keep the top gold frame facing outward.
4. Tighten the corners evenly and inspect for bowing or contact with narrow features.
5. Check rail, nut, and case-bottom clearance in the actual case.
6. Record photos and review color, sight lines, and hardware coverage.

Do not mix standard and wide Kumiko. Keep the narrowing Coral and Fault layers in the intended order.

## Record the physical result

Record spacer, screw, washer, and nut part numbers; actual stack height; board bow; and case fit. Review exposed routed edges and thin strands for contact with hands or cables.
