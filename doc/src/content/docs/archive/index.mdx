The original Revision 5 handoff supplied 28 documentation pages, source geometry and review evidence. Those pages have been reconciled into the maintained site. Historical review findings are labeled as historical on the current manufacturing pages; newer native/CAM and completed-order evidence takes precedence for current status.

- [Continue design work](/docs/guides/continuation/) — source boundaries and evidence sequence.
- [References and attribution](/docs/overview/references/) — original bibliography and notices.
- [Original overlay at the parent commit](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/tree/f31c49d3cad0364033c18d4f91d07139ea4872f5/artwork-resources/pcb-art/docs-overlay/src/content/docs) — read-only historical text.
- [Page-by-page reconciliation](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/blob/main/doc/data/route-migration.json) — all 28 destinations and preserved content.
- [Historical copper/mask screen](/docs/manufacturing/copper-review/#historical-import-review) and [Kumiko aperture screen](/docs/manufacturing/kumiko-routing/#historical-import-review) — original measurements with current evidence above them.

The [completed four-series manufacturing archive](/docs/manufacturing/order/) is a different kind of archive: frozen native/CAM/ZIP bytes and completion/provenance metadata, retained unchanged in ordinary Git. Its external downloads are pinned to the recorded parent commit. This website does not reproduce the whole archive.
