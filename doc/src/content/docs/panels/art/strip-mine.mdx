## Pattern, stack and evidence

Strip Mine is a decorative 20HP blank with nine stacked PCBs. The top carries exposed ENIG contour artwork and building clusters around an open pit; alternating black/red layers retreat to a blue lake. The selected scene is **pattern 5, Strata/blobs, with buildings on**. Nine 1.6 mm boards and eight 3 mm gaps give a nominal 38.4 mm stack, excluding fasteners.

The [native project README](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/blob/main/panels/art-strip-mine/README.md) records the layer sequence, generator and earlier order instructions. The owner reports successful assembly using the purchased M3 ×45 mm screws, 3 mm spacers and M3 nuts. See the [assembly evidence and comparison](/docs/guides/assembly/stack/) for the limits of that report. Strip Mine is separate from the newer four-series completed archive.

## Files and interpretation

Native `.kicad_pcb` files are editable board layouts; `.kicad_pro` files carry project settings. PNGs are source-derived directory previews, not CAM approval. Alternate, fabrication-sheet and test boards are labeled separately in the inventory below. Repository downloads are external links; only the selected files on [3D & Files](/docs/models/) are indexed locally.
