title: "Artwork families"
---

Layered panels create depth with separate native PCBs and spacers. Earlier single-panel artwork remains available alongside them.

![Five Revision 5 visual concepts](/assets/pcb-art/images/pcb-art-previews-overview.png)

[Explore the interactive 3D scenes](/docs/models/)

- [Spider Nest](./spider-nest/) — eight-layer web over a red bottom.
- [Coral Vault](./coral-vault/) — eight layers of unevenly retreating cavities.
- [Fault Line](./fault-line/) — nine layers of angular and rounded cliffs.
- [Kumiko Void](./kumiko-void/) — selected wide set and complete standard reference alternative.
- [Woven Maze](./woven-maze/) — nine layers with seven distinct maze paths; outside the completed order.
- [Strip Mine](./strip-mine/) — nine-layer predecessor, selected pattern 5, Strata/blobs.
- [Takazudo Modular](./takazudo-modular/) — earlier 10HP artwork.
- [Sun](./sun/) — earlier 12HP artwork.
- [UFO](./ufo/) — original and v2 revisions.

The [completed four-series order](/docs/manufacturing/order/) covers Spider Nest, Coral Vault, Fault Line and Kumiko Void wide. Strip Mine has its own earlier order and assembly evidence. Woven Maze and standard Kumiko retain their historical/reference identities.
