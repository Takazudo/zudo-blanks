Kumiko combines an asanoha lattice with black, red, and green layers. On black layers 1, 4, and 7, the flat gold and black surfaces create the visual shadow; no raised metal parts are specified. The standard option preserves the earlier opening. The selected wide option extends the same lattice toward the perimeter and increases top opening area by about 43.8% relative to standard.

![Standard and wide opening comparison](/assets/pcb-art/images/kumiko-void-variants.png)

<a href="/viewers/pcb-art/?design=kumiko-void&amp;variant=standard">Standard (reference only)</a> · <a href="/viewers/pcb-art/?design=kumiko-void&amp;variant=wide">Wide (selected)</a>

## Treat the variants as whole sets

The selected set uses wide Kumiko. Its nine standard boards are retained under panels/art-kumiko-void/alternatives/standard. Replace all nine layers together if choosing standard; do not mix layers from the two variants. Revision 5 borders are present on both tops.

## Selected wide and reference standard

The wide nine-board set was locally adapted for the chosen 1.0 mm cutter and checked in native KiCad and CAM. The standard nine-board alternative remains reference-only and was not part of the selected release checks. Do not mix variants within one stack. [Read the policy and indexed corrections](/docs/manufacturing/kumiko-routing/).

## Files and interpretation

Native `.kicad_pcb` files are editable board layouts; `.kicad_pro` files carry project settings. PNGs are source-derived directory previews, not CAM approval. Alternate, fabrication-sheet and test boards are labeled separately in the inventory below. Repository downloads are external links; only the selected files on [3D & Files](/docs/models/) are indexed locally.
