Fault Line is a tall, asymmetric canyon. Its side ledges reach different heights; black layers have angular cliffs while red layers have worn, rounded cliffs. Retreat varies locally. The top opening grew about 38.1% from Revision 3 to Revision 4. Top copper adds terrain lines and narrow fractures, with a Revision 5 frame and hole rims. The lower eight boards are mask-only.

## Keep each layer connected

The left and right portions of every layer must remain connected through its upper and lower bridges. Splitting a layer into separate boards held together by screws would violate the one-PCB-per-layer design.

Opening area decreases with depth and remains nested, but the geometry is not a uniform offset. Keep local changes irregular and check that deep openings do not create floating PCB fragments.

## Files and interpretation

Native `.kicad_pcb` files are editable board layouts; `.kicad_pro` files carry project settings. PNGs are source-derived directory previews, not CAM approval. Alternate, fabrication-sheet and test boards are labeled separately in the inventory below.
