The original handoff bibliography is retained in [RESEARCH_SOURCES.json](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/blob/main/artwork-resources/pcb-art/documentation/RESEARCH_SOURCES.json), with [third-party notices](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/blob/main/artwork-resources/pcb-art/preview-source/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt). Source files, comparison images and native KiCad projects retain their repository provenance. Curated web assets are staged copies of those committed sources.

[Strip Mine](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/tree/main/panels/art-strip-mine) supplies the layered-panel predecessor and stack reference. [Favites pentagona](https://www.coralsoftheworld.org/species_factsheets/species_factsheet_summary/favites-pentagona/) and [Smithsonian Ocean's coral overview](https://ocean.si.edu/ocean-life/invertebrates/corals-and-coral-reefs) informed Coral Vault's abstract shapes. Design dimensions and layer sequences come from the local geometry and manifests, not those visual references.

The handoff also cited [KiCad board format](https://dev-docs.kicad.org/en/file-formats/sexpr-pcb/), [KiCad common graphics and pads](https://dev-docs.kicad.org/en/file-formats/sexpr-intro/), [JLCPCB capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities), [surface finishes](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/jlcpcb-surface-finish), and [NPTH design guidance](https://jlcpcb.com/blog/npth-design-guide). The [frozen manufacturing policy](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/blob/main/artwork-resources/pcb-art/manufacturing/README.md) records which process assumptions were chosen after checking official sources. Recheck current supplier rules and obtain a quote before any order; a citation is not acceptance.
