The frozen Revision 5 source includes geometry and native-export screens. Later local work adapted the 43 selected boards for the chosen router and copper/mask rules, ran targeted geometry checks, and checked native KiCad 10 DRC and CAM outputs. Five grouped lower sheets received separate local native/CAM checks. The nine standard Kumiko boards remain unchanged reference designs.

An erosion screen or a clean KiCad DRC does not prove material strength, supplier process compatibility or physical fit. The exhaustive width certificate remains opt-in and has not passed. Factory CAM, quote, finish availability and physical assembly are pending.
