## Known review items

Kumiko has 64 openings below the assumed router entry size and a wide-top ligament about 0.1166 mm wide. The export candidate clips visible gold; some copper and mask gaps are also very narrow. Use the measured locations in the review records and decide any design change with the chosen process.

| Topic | Check | Evidence to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tool diameter and interior radius | Minimum manufacturable strands, lattice, paths, and walls | Supplier response and revised drawing |
| Copper and mask clearance | Distances from outside edges, cutouts, and NPTH holes | Applied rules and DRC |
| Exposed ENIG | Top borders, hole rims, and full-gold layers | Copper/mask CAM image |
| Narrow supports | Local strength and handling | Prototype and assembly review |
| Mask colors | Availability and actual hue, including purple and yellow | Supplier specification or sample |
| Mask-only boards | Accepted empty decorative copper layers and finish | Supplier confirmation and quote |
| Drills and slots | NPTH treatment, slot output, drill origin | CAM output and supplier confirmation |
| Spacers and screws | Actual sizes, stack length, nut allowance, rail clearance | Part numbers and trial assembly |
| Kumiko variant | Standard or wide as a complete set | Selected manifest |

If a change is needed, record board ID, layer, coordinates, reason, and geometry delta. Plan whether the generator or KiCad production copy becomes the source of truth.

A planar connectivity check does not establish physical strength. A prototype is needed to assess breakage, flex, edge feel, screw load, stack sight lines, and color. These items remain pending; the imported files are not factory-accepted.
