## Process-dependent issues flagged

The incoming geometry review found decorative openings too small for the assumed 1.0 mm circular router: 8 in standard Kumiko and 56 in wide Kumiko. The original large-opening design geometry is retained. These features have not been silently filled or enlarged.

Confirm the actual fabrication process and router. Possible local decisions include enlarging an opening, combining adjacent openings, or closing a tiny opening. Use a reviewed, versioned board for any manufacturing adaptation.

The CSV issue ID, variant, layer, and original JSON hole index identify each location. Standard layer 3 includes a 0.075 mm slit. A wide opening has a maximum inscribed-circle diameter near 0.0143 mm. Wide top openings also leave a local ligament about 0.1166 mm wide near opening indices 68 and 101, around coordinates (93.5172, 26.8) and (93.4162, 26.7417) mm.

A 0.5 mm inward-offset connectivity result does not guarantee every local feature is at least 1.0 mm wide. The same review did not flag other designs for this particular whole-opening router screen, but that does not establish all corners, narrow features, or physical strength as acceptable.
