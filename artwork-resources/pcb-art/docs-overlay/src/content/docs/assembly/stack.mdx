## Four-series hardware counts

One artwork means one complete stack, including its top PCB. The four ordered series use the following counts, excluding spares and rack/rail mounting hardware:

| Artwork | Boards | Gaps | M3 ×45 mm screws | 3 mm spacers | M3 nuts | Boards plus gaps |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Coral Vault | 8 | 7 | 4 | 28 | 4 | 33.8 mm |
| Fault Line | 9 | 8 | 4 | 32 | 4 | 38.4 mm |
| Kumiko Void wide | 9 | 8 | 4 | 32 | 4 | 38.4 mm |
| Spider Nest | 8 | 7 | 4 | 28 | 4 | 33.8 mm |

All 34 original native boards were checked: each has four aligned Ø3.2 mm stack holes. One long screw passes through all layers at each axis and is secured by one rear nut. Each gap takes one 3 mm spacer on each of the four axes, so **spacers per artwork = 4 × (board layers − 1)**. Decorative apertures and the four top-only rail slots are separate from these stack holes; their mounting hardware is excluded. See [the mounting coordinates](../engineering/dimensions.mdx).

A **four-series combined set** contains one complete artwork of each series: **16 screws, 120 spacers and 16 nuts**. The ordered **25 combined sets** (25 artworks per series, 100 artworks total) require **400 screws, 3,000 spacers and 400 nuts**, excluding spares.

The owner reports that these exact M3 ×45 mm screws, M3-compatible 3 mm spacers and M3 nuts have already been purchased and successfully used to assemble Strip Mine. This records owner-reported experience with the parts. Assembly results for the four new series have not yet been recorded. With the same hardware and no added plates or changed washer arrangement, the 45 mm length has the same nominal reach as the working Strip Mine assembly for Fault Line and Kumiko Void wide: all three use nine 1.6 mm boards and eight 3 mm gaps, totaling 38.4 mm. Coral Vault and Spider Nest total 33.8 mm, which is 4.6 mm thinner.

The [Strip Mine hardware record](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-blanks/blob/daca5d44e5b6ec382481a1c1e9a3e634b04381ea/panels/art-strip-mine/README.md#L35-L45) calculates screw length **under the head**, with an assumed 2.4 mm nut. On that basis, 45 mm leaves 6.6 mm beyond a nine-board stack (4.2 mm beyond the assumed nut), or 11.2 mm beyond an eight-board stack (8.8 mm beyond the assumed nut), before any washers. The actual purchased screw head type, washer arrangement and nut dimensions are not recorded; the comparison above uses the same parts the owner already assembled, rather than assuming a different screw-length convention.

The board/gap heights above use 1.6 mm PCBs and 3.0 mm gaps, excluding heads, nuts and washers. The existing preview assumes 6 mm spacer outside diameter.

## Assembly sequence

1. Sort PCBs by design, original board ID, layer number, and side.
2. Stack from the bottom up, adding one 3 mm gap and aligning all four holes.
3. Check the color sequence and keep the top gold frame facing outward.
4. Tighten the corners evenly and inspect for bowing or contact with narrow features.
5. Check rail, nut, and case-bottom clearance in the actual case.
6. Record photos and review color, sight lines, and hardware coverage.

Do not mix standard and wide Kumiko. Keep the narrowing Coral and Fault layers in the intended order.

## Record the physical result

Record spacer, screw, washer, and nut part numbers; actual stack height; board bow; and case fit. Review exposed routed edges and thin strands for contact with hands or cables.
