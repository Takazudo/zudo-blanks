## Decisions to preserve

- The top board is 101.3 × 128.5 mm; lower boards are 101.3 × 94.3 mm.
- Boards are 1.6 mm thick and separated by 3.0 mm spacers. Every layer remains one connected physical PCB.
- The openings reach toward the perimeter; do not reduce the designs to a small centered square.
- The gold outer frame and hole rims appear on top boards only.
- Spider Nest and Kumiko Void use angular decorative outlines. Functional screw holes and slots keep their specified shapes.
- Kumiko gold and black are flat artwork, not raised metal bars.
- Colors and ENIG layers are listed in [the finish table](../engineering/finishes.mdx).

## Kumiko selection

The default set uses wide Kumiko in panels/art-kumiko-void. The nine standard boards in panels/art-kumiko-void/alternatives/standard replace all nine wide boards as a set. Do not mix layers between variants. Both versions share the same color and ENIG sequence.
