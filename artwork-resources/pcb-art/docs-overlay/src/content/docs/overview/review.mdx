## Evidence completed in the handoff

Geometry generation checks each board's connected material, mounting supports, rail restrictions, and opening bounds. A 0.5 mm inward-offset check screens for some narrow necks. It does not prove minimum cut width, mechanical strength, toolpath quality, or tolerance.

The Revision 5 viewer was checked for offline rendering and geometry presentation. Its colors approximate finishes on screen; use physical samples for color decisions. A separate S-expression audit compares all 52 board outlines, holes, copper, and mask shapes against the source geometry. The retained report states that KiCad itself was not run.
