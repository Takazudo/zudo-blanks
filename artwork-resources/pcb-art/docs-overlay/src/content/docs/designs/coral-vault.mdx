Six large flowing cavities and four smaller openings start the view. Walls retreat by varying amounts with depth, and some cavities split as partitions appear. This avoids a repeated scaled-copy effect. The design abstracts cup, shared-wall, and radial-septum forms of colonial coral such as [Favites pentagona](https://www.coralsoftheworld.org/species_factsheets/species_factsheet_summary/favites-pentagona/); it is not a model of one organism.

The top copper spreads cup and radial motifs across the remaining face. Revision 5 adds the gold frame and hole rims. Layer 4 is the only full-gold lower layer. The top opening grew about 62.0% from Revision 3.

## Layering constraints

Combined decorative aperture area decreases with depth, and every deeper aperture remains inside the preceding opening. Hole counts may change. Keep each layer connected and ensure top openings do not extend beyond lower-board material.
